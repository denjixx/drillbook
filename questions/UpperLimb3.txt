Q: A 35-year-old cyclist falls onto his shoulder and fractures the middle third of the clavicle. Fortunately, the subclavian vessels and the trunks of the brachial plexus behind the bone are not damaged. Which muscle provides a protective cushion in this situation?

B) Subclavius
C) Deltoid
D) Pectoralis minor
E) Sternocleidomastoid
Answer: B
Explanation: The subclavius lies in the subclavian groove under the clavicle, in front of the subclavian vessels and brachial plexus trunks, acting as a cushion that protects them in fractures. The clavipectoral fascia encloses it. [Chaurasia]
