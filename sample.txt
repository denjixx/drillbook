Q: Which organelle is the main site of ATP production in a eukaryotic cell?
A) Ribosome
B) Mitochondrion
C) Golgi apparatus
D) Lysosome
Answer: B
Explanation: Mitochondria carry out oxidative phosphorylation, which produces most of the cell's ATP.

Q: What is the derivative of x^2?
A) x
B) 2
C) 2x
D) x^2/2
Answer: C
Explanation: The power rule gives d/dx x^n = n·x^(n-1), so d/dx x^2 = 2x.
